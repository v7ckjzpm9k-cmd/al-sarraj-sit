About Al-Sarraj Company

Al-Sarraj Company for Engineering Architecture is a trusted name in engineering contracting and general trading, committed to delivering high-quality solutions with precision, professionalism, and long-term value. Established in 2024, the company was founded with a clear vision: to bring together engineering expertise, modern planning, and practical execution under one unified standard of excellence.

From the beginning, Al-Sarraj Company has focused on building strong relationships with clients through honesty, reliability, and consistent performance. We believe that every project is more than just a construction task — it is a responsibility that requires dedication, technical knowledge, and careful attention to detail.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide integrated engineering and contracting services that meet the highest standards of quality, safety, and efficiency. We aim to transform ideas into well-executed projects by combining innovative thinking, skilled workmanship, and smart management.

We are dedicated to serving our clients with solutions that are both practical and forward-thinking, ensuring that every project reflects professionalism, durability, and architectural value.

Our Vision

Our vision is to expand with confidence and become one of the leading companies in engineering and contracting by adopting environmentally friendly technologies and intelligent ideas that support sustainable development.

We strive to create a future where modern engineering, elegant design, and responsible execution work together to serve communities and inspire progress.

Why Choose Us

Choosing Al-Sarraj Company means choosing commitment, quality, and a team that understands the importance of delivering work on time and according to specifications. Our team includes specialists in architecture, civil engineering, electrical systems, and mechanical works, allowing us to manage projects with complete coordination.

We do not simply execute projects — we develop them with care, follow them with responsibility, and deliver them with pride. Our approach is built on transparency, continuous improvement, and respect for the client’s goals.

Our Values

Quality, integrity, commitment, and innovation are the core values that define our work. We believe that strong results come from strong principles, and that every successful project begins with trust and ends with satisfaction.

We maintain a professional standard in every stage of our work, from planning and design to implementation and final delivery, ensuring that each detail contributes to the overall success of the project.

Our Capability

Al-Sarraj Company offers a wide range of engineering and general contracting services, including engineering design, project management, engineering supervision, electrical and mechanical works, construction works, engineering consulting, maintenance, and operation.

With a growing reputation in Baghdad, Iraq, and a strong commitment to excellence, we continue to deliver solutions that combine technical strength with aesthetic quality and functional performance.

Our Story

Al-Sarraj Company for Engineering Architecture was created with the belief that engineering is not only about construction, but about shaping spaces, improving functionality, and creating lasting value. Our company stands on the foundation of professional expertise, modern methods, and a deep understanding of client expectations. We are proud to serve our clients with a style of work that combines elegance, strength, and responsibility.

Through our projects, we aim to reflect a refined engineering identity that values accuracy in execution, clarity in communication, and excellence in results. We continue to grow by embracing smart ideas, sustainable thinking, and a strong dedication to the future of architecture and construction.
